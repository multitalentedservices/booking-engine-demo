Chapter 01 · Slot logic

What the engine actually does

Slot logic, not a picture of slots

Slots are generated from business hours, each resource's own hours and days off, the service duration plus its buffer, bookings already taken, and a two-hour lead-time rule. A slot exists only when a real resource is working and free.

Confirmations with references

Every completed booking gets a reference code, is saved in the browser, appears in the admin view, and offers a WhatsApp confirmation link with the details prefilled.

An admin view that bites back

Admin is not a screenshot: filter by date, resource or status, and cancelling a booking there frees its slot in the booking flow. Try it — book, cancel in Admin, then watch the slot return.

Chapter 03 · The flow

How a booking flows

1 · Choose

Service, then a named resource or First available, then a date. Closed days and past dates are rejected with the reason named.

2 · Pick a slot

Only slots a working, free resource can actually serve are enabled. Disabled slots say why — already booked, not working, or too soon.

3 · Confirm

Details are validated step by step, errors clear as you fix them, and the confirmation carries a reference you can find again in Admin.
