How it works

Configuration is the product

A client does not buy pages — they configure one object. Everything on the Book and Admin pages is derived from it. Below is the engine's real demo configuration, not marketing copy.

Services

Each service carries a name, duration, price and buffer. The buffer is protected time after the appointment: a 45-minute haircut with a 10-minute buffer blocks 55 minutes of the diary, so the next slot must fit the whole block before closing.

Resources

A resource is a person or room with its own weekly hours and days off. In the salon skin Mahnoor Ali is off on Tuesdays and takes a day off on 26 November 2026; in the clinic skin Dr Omar Farooq works afternoons only and never on Mondays. The slot grid obeys all of it.

Business hours & closed days

Business hours set the outer window per weekday; a resource's hours narrow it further. All three demo businesses close on Sundays — pick a Sunday in Book and the engine names the closure instead of showing an empty grid.

Slot interval & lead time

The interval is how often candidate slots start: 30 minutes in the engine and salon skins, 20 minutes in the clinic skin. The lead-time rule blocks anything sooner than two hours after the demo clock (Tuesday 24 November 2026, 09:00).

The rules, in order

  1. A candidate slot starts on the interval inside business hours.
  2. It survives only if the service plus buffer fits before closing.
  3. It is bookable only if the chosen resource (or, for First available, at least one resource) is working then, is not on a day off, and has no overlapping booking.
  4. Deterministic demo bookings and bookings saved in this browser both block slots; cancelling in Admin unblocks them.
  5. Past slots and slots inside the lead-time window are disabled with the reason printed on the slot itself.

What a real deployment would add

This demo runs entirely in the browser with demo data, by design. A client deployment would connect the same config and rules to a real database, staff logins, payment and reminder channels. None of that is connected here, and nothing on this site sends data anywhere.
